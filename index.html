Research collections

The work is organised into four collections. Each dataset keeps its source document, page and table, and every value carries an explicit verification status.

COLLECTION 01

Indonesia Judiciary Open Data

Indonesia Master Court Registry 2026 (v0.9)

Relational registry of Indonesian courts across the four judicial environments, reconciled against Mahkamah Agung RI decrees and 2026 PERKUSI civil-execution statistics. 906 courts verified individually; remaining courts are flagged as pending verification.

DatasetKaggleGitHubPublic

Mahkamah Agung RI Financial Reconciliation, FY2023–FY2025

Structured tables from the audited financial statements of Mahkamah Agung RI: budget execution, court escrow funds, grants, state-loss receivables and capital projects, each with a reconciliation report.

Dataset ×3FY2023FY2024FY2025Kaggle 2023Kaggle 2024Kaggle 2025Public

Indonesian Court Transparency Scorecard

Judiciary open-data tables covering 2006–2025: annual tables, strategic indicators and source-audit registers, with layouts that differ between years. The 2026–2028 file is a model projection, not reported data.

DatasetKaggleGitHubPublic

Indonesian Court Transparency EDA

Python notebook: file inventory, Supreme Court caseload for the years with comparable layouts (reported vs computed clearance), and the labelled 2026–2028 projection.

NotebookKagglePublic

Technology

Python and pandas for extraction and validation. Retrieval-augmented generation over legal documents, with Qdrant for vector search and Neo4j for legal knowledge graphs. GitHub Actions for automated validation. The registry's sync pipelines are in the registry repository.

Verification-first method

SourceEach value keeps its source URL, report year, page and table.
StatusEach value is marked VERIFIED, PARTIALLY_VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED or CONFLICT. Running code successfully is never treated as verification.
Labelled transformationsNormalised, calculated or projected values are labelled and kept apart from reported values.
PrivacyPersonal and protected case information is removed or redacted before release.

About

Cecep Mustafa researches Indonesian judicial open data, rule of law and judicial governance, computational legal research, and AI-enabled institutional knowledge. His work is carried out with the Indonesian Judicial College (IJC). He builds reproducible, source-verified datasets and analytical workflows that connect law, data science and intelligent justice.
